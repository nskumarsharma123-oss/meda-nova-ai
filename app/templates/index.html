⚠️ Medical Research & Safety Declarations

  • Non-Diagnostic: MedNova AI does NOT issue definitive medical diagnoses or confirm disease presence.
  • No Treatment Advice: The application will never recommend initiating, pausing, or altering medications.
  • Synthetic Data: For demonstration and evaluation, users must submit only de-identified or synthetic patient profiles.
  • Clinician In The Loop: Rare disease differential analysis is an aid for licensed clinical geneticists and specialists.
